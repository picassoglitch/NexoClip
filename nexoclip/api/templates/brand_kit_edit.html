{# How clipping actually works — concrete worked examples so the operator doesn't have to reverse-engineer "forward" vs "retroactive" from the field labels. #}
NexoClip listens to your stream's audio. When the transcript matches one of these phrases, a clip is extracted around that timestamp:
Forward — for things you anticipate
"watch this — clipea esto" → clip = next 30s
phrase +30s
Use for moments you can predict before they happen.
Retroactive — for moments that just ended
"clipeaste eso jajaja" → clip = previous 60s
−60s phrase
The natural one — the funny moment ends, then you say it.
Phrases are matched case-insensitively with fuzzy tolerance (Whisper transcription is imperfect). Add as many variants as you want — one per line. They're added on top of the tenant-wide defaults (your tenant already has the spec defaults wired in: clipea esto, clipeaste eso, and the EN equivalents).
